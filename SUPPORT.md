Support contact, hours and any support agreement: **placeholder, to be provided by Creative Global Financial Solutions.**

When you report a problem, include:

- the version (`deasys-server version`),
- the screen or command, and the exact message,
- the time it happened,
- the relevant lines of `deasys.log`, with names and addresses removed.

Never send passwords, the `jwt_key`, the database connection string, or a backup file.
